About

My name is Joachim Wéry. I am a developer and web designer with three years of experience.

Passionate about media and digital, I earned a bachelor's degree in multimedia writing, then a master's degree in transmedia architecture. To stay on the cutting edge, I completed an intensive six-month web design course before specializing in .NET development to sharpen my back-end skills.

Versatile and solution-oriented, I work across web development, integration, security, and software according to each project's needs.

Naturally curious, I enjoy exploring new ideas and technologies to tackle creative and technical challenges. I am currently looking for new professional opportunities and would be happy to discuss your projects.
